00 / THE COLLECTIVE

One group.
Many directions.

PandaHat is built around a research habit, not one permanent topic.

We ask how intelligent systems learn, where they fail, what evidence supports their behavior, and how those results can be reproduced. Projects can change without changing the identity of the group.
